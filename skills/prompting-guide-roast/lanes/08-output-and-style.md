Digest entries: C06, O04, O10.
Hunt in contracts for reports, specs, summaries and final messages: no calibration of length or shape for the reader; numeric word or bullet caps where audience framing would serve; templates whose single sample will be over-indexed; final messages that do not lead with the outcome for a reader who sees only the last message.
When one contract is read by models that need opposite fixes (a Claude Fable writer and a GPT-6 Astra writer), report it as one finding with the conflict stated, and recommend describing the reader and shape instead of adding or removing formatting bans.

## claude
Claude writers: Opus writes long documents, so flag a deliverable with no length calibration. Fable leans into dense, mannered prose and into formatting, so flag missing density guidance and carried-over anti-formatting rules. Flag invitations to narrate corrections (C06).

## openai
GPT-6 Astra writers, and Sol and Luna, which share its style: flag a prose deliverable with no style spec. Astra defaults to lists, tables, stock phrases ("delve", "leverage", "it's worth noting", "Bottom Line:"), contrastive "X, not Y" framing and invented compound labels (O04). Preamble and status-update instructions are at most low-confidence findings, because the guidance has flipped across generations (O10).
